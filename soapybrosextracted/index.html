FAQ

Before your visit.

The essentials on preparing your vehicle, timing, and payment. Anything else, call or text 319-406-6159.

What do I need to do to get ready?
Please remove personal belongings from the vehicle, or place them in the center console or glove box. We also need access to water and electricity.
How long does a detail take?
An interior detail takes about 1 hour 30 minutes, and an exterior detail takes about 45 minutes. Depending on the condition of the vehicle, it may take longer.
What if my car is excessively dirty?
Additional charges may apply depending on the severity. We confirm the final price once we have seen the vehicle.
How do I pay?
We accept cash, Apple Pay, Venmo, Zelle, and Cash App.
Do I need to be present?
No. As long as water and power are accessible and we have your keys, you do not need to be present.
