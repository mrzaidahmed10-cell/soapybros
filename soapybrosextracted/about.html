Soapy Bros is a locally owned mobile detailing business founded by three high school students in Iowa City.

We started Soapy Bros with a simple goal: provide convenient, reliable car detailing while building something of our own. What began as an idea has grown into a business serving customers throughout Iowa City, Coralville, and North Liberty.

We believe a great detail comes down to the little things. From the hard-to-reach crevices to the final wipe-down, we take pride in delivering quality work and treating every vehicle with care.

We’re young entrepreneurs, but we take our work seriously. When you book with Soapy Bros, you’re supporting a local business built on honesty, quality, and attention to detail.
