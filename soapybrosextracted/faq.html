Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know before your visit. If your question is not here, call or text us.

What hours do you work?
Appointments are available between 8 AM and 8 PM, with a 3-hour window. Choose the time that suits you when you book.
How do I book?
Choose a date and time on the booking page, or call or text 319-406-6159. We confirm your time by phone or text.
How much does a detail cost?
Starting quotes depend on your vehicle type and the service you choose, beginning at $40 for a sedan exterior. We confirm the final price once we have seen the vehicle.
Which areas do you serve?
We serve Iowa City, Coralville, and North Liberty. If you are just outside these areas, reach out and we may still be able to help.
What's included in a detail?
Interior: a thorough vacuum, thorough vacuuming and wiping of seats, dashboard and console, door panels and interior surfaces, cup holders and crevices, interior windows, and floor mats. Exterior: a hand wash of the entire vehicle, bug removal, wheel and tire cleaning, exterior window cleaning, removal of water spots, door jambs, and a dry, streak-free finish.
What do I need to do to get ready?
Please remove personal belongings from the vehicle, or place them in the center console or glove box. We also need access to water and electricity.
How long does a detail take?
An interior detail takes about 1 hour 30 minutes, and an exterior detail takes about 45 minutes. Depending on the condition of the vehicle, it may take longer.
What if my car is excessively dirty?
Additional charges may apply depending on the severity. We confirm the final price once we have seen the vehicle.
How do I pay?
We accept cash, Apple Pay, Venmo, Zelle, and Cash App.
Do I need to be present?
No. As long as water and power are accessible and we have your keys, you do not need to be present.
